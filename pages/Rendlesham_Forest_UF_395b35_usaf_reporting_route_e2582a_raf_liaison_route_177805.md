## Introduction

The route by which the [Rendlesham Forest]({{ 'rendlesham-forest/' | relative_url }}) report reached Whitehall was neither mysterious nor exceptional. It followed a British-American liaison channel that existed because RAF Woodbridge and RAF [Bentwaters]({{ 'bentwaters/' | relative_url }}) were Royal Air Force stations operated by the United States Air Force under long-standing defence agreements. When Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt wrote his memorandum about the December 1980 sightings, he did not send it directly into a public UFO investigation system. Instead, the report moved through the RAF liaison structure that connected the American bases in Suffolk with the Ministry of Defence (MoD) in London. ## Why the Memo Went Through RAF Channels

Although the witnesses and many of the officers involved were American, the events occurred at facilities located in the United Kingdom. RAF Woodbridge and RAF Bentwaters were USAF-operated bases, but they remained part of the British defence estate. As a result, communication with the British government frequently passed through designated RAF liaison officers whose job was to bridge the two command systems. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Georgina%20Bruni%20-%20You%20Can%27t%20Tell%20the%20People.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avalon LibraryThe Definitive Account of the Rendlesham Forest UFO MysteryROLE: Squadron Leader Donald Moreland was the British liaison of...</span></span></span>

Halt's memorandum, dated 13 January 1981 and titled *[Unexplained]({{ 'unexplained/' | relative_url }}) Lights*, was produced as an official account of the sightings. Rather than being dispatched directly to ministers or intelligence agencies, it entered the established liaison process. Surviving records show that the memorandum was routed to the RAF liaison office, creating the formal connection between the American base command and the British Ministry of Defence. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File%3AHalt_Memorandum.jpg" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: commons.wikimedia.org">[Wikimedia Commons+2The Black Vault Documents]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">commons.wikimedia.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Commons File:Halt Memorandum.jpg</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Wikimedia CommonsFile:Halt Memorandum.jpg - Wikimedia CommonsEnglish: Memorandum by Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt to the British Minist...</span></span></span>

This distinction matters because the reporting route reflected administrative responsibility rather than investigative enthusiasm. The existence of a reporting channel ensured that unusual events could be brought to the attention of British authorities. It did not automatically trigger a dedicated investigative programme. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://hansard.parliament.uk/Lords/1997-10-14/debates/96394ce5-f0db-4448-af50-43a69fbf3c57/LieutenantColonelCharlesHaltMemorandum" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: hansard.parliament.uk">[Hansard]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">hansard.parliament.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Hansard Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt: Memorandum - Hansard14 Oct 1997 — The memorandum, which reported observations of unusual lights i...</span></span></span>

## Donald Moreland's Forwarding Role

The key British figure in the handoff was Squadron Leader Donald H. Moreland, the RAF liaison officer attached to the bases. Multiple released documents and later interviews identify him as the officer who forwarded Halt's report to the Ministry of Defence. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://drdavidclarke.co.uk/tag/donald-moreland/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: drdavidclarke.co.uk">[Dr. David Clarke+2The Guardian]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">drdavidclarke.co.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Dr. David Clarke Donald Moreland</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Dr. David ClarkeDonald Moreland &#124; - Dr David Clarke26 Dec 2020 — Leader Donald Moreland, the British liaison officer. He forwarded the ac...</span></span></span>

Moreland's covering letter has become one of the most important documents in reconstructing the reporting chain. Dated 15 January 1981, it accompanied Halt's memorandum and informed the MoD that he was forwarding a report concerning "some mysterious sightings in the Rendlesham Forest near RAF Woodbridge." He sent it onward "for your information and action as considered necessary." <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Georgina%20Bruni%20-%20You%20Can%27t%20Tell%20the%20People.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avalon LibraryThe Definitive Account of the Rendlesham Forest UFO MysteryROLE: Squadron Leader Donald Moreland was the British liaison of...</span></span></span>

The wording is revealing. Moreland was not claiming that an extraterrestrial craft had landed, nor was he requesting a special inquiry. He was forwarding information through an established channel and leaving any further action to the receiving department. The language is administrative and procedural, consistent with routine liaison work rather than crisis escalation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Georgina%20Bruni%20-%20You%20Can%27t%20Tell%20the%20People.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avalon LibraryThe Definitive Account of the Rendlesham Forest UFO MysteryROLE: Squadron Leader Donald Moreland was the British liaison of...</span></span></span>

His letter also shows that the communication was addressed to DS8a, part of Defence Secretariat 8, the branch that handled UFO-related correspondence within the Ministry of Defence at the time. A copy was sent through RAF liaison structures at RAF Mildenhall, demonstrating that the report moved through recognised military-administrative pathways rather than an ad hoc chain created specifically for the incident. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://avalonlibrary.net/Rendlesham_Forest_incident_1980/Georgina%20Bruni%20-%20You%20Can%27t%20Tell%20the%20People.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: avalonlibrary.net">[Avalon Library]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">avalonlibrary.net</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Avalon LibraryThe Definitive Account of the Rendlesham Forest UFO MysteryROLE: Squadron Leader Donald Moreland was the British liaison of...</span></span></span>

## What Happened Once the Report Reached Whitehall?

The surviving evidence indicates that the memorandum was reviewed within the MoD system, particularly by officials concerned with air-defence implications. The central question was not whether the observations were extraordinary in a popular sense. ## What the Liaison Route Says About Official Ownership

One of the most persistent questions surrounding Rendlesham is whether the incident was primarily an American matter or a British one. The liaison route provides a [partial answer]({{ 'partial-answer/' | relative_url }}): it was both, but in different ways.

The sightings originated within a USAF operational environment and were documented by American personnel.
