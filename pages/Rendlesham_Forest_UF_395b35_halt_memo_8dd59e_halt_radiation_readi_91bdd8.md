## Introduction

The radiation readings recorded by Lieutenant Colonel Charles Halt are among the most frequently cited pieces of physical evidence from the [Rendlesham Forest]({{ 'rendlesham-forest/' | relative_url }}) incident. They appear in Halt’s January 1981 memorandum to the Ministry of Defence and are often presented as proof that something extraordinary landed in the forest. The memo itself, however, supports a much narrower conclusion.

What the readings show is that measurable radiation levels were recorded at specific ground marks and on part of a nearby tree. What they do not show is the source of that radiation, whether it was unusual for the area, or whether it was connected to a landed craft. The significance of the measurements depends heavily on the instrument used, the baseline levels available for comparison, and the absence of formal scientific controls. As a result, the readings remain part of the Rendlesham story, but not a decisive proof of what happened there. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

## What the Memo Says About the Ground Readings

Halt's memorandum records that three shallow depressions were found at a location where personnel believed an object had been observed. According to the memo, radiation checks conducted the following night produced beta/gamma readings of 0.1 milliroentgens, with peak readings in the depressions and near the centre of the triangular pattern they formed. The memo also notes readings of roughly 0.05–0.07 on one side of a nearby tree facing the depressions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

These details matter because they are among the few physical measurements documented in the official record. Unlike witness recollections added years later, the readings were reported by a serving deputy base commander shortly after the event and became part of the Ministry of Defence file. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

However, the memo reports the measurements without interpreting them. It does not state that the readings were dangerous, extraordinary, or evidence of a spacecraft. It records observations rather than conclusions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

## Why the Numbers Need Context

The central problem is that a radiation reading by itself says very little. To know whether a measurement is significant, investigators need reliable [background]({{ 'background/' | relative_url }}) levels, calibrated instruments, repeat measurements and controls from comparable locations.

Accounts based on the Halt investigation indicate that readings around 0.07 milliroentgens per hour were found in the depressions, while nearby areas measured approximately 0.03–0.04 milliroentgens per hour. A similar elevated reading was reportedly detected elsewhere in the forest, away from the alleged [landing site]({{ 'landing-site/' | relative_url }}). <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest incident</span></span></span>

Supporters of the UFO interpretation have argued that the higher readings at the depressions suggest a physical event occurred there. Critics counter that naturally varying background radiation can produce differences of this kind and that finding comparable readings elsewhere weakens the claim that the measurements uniquely identify a landing location. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rendlesham_Forest_incident" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia+2Morbid Kuriosity]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Rendlesham Forest incident</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Rendlesham Forest incident</span></span></span>

Another issue concerns the measuring equipment. The investigation used an AN/PDR-27 military radiation survey meter. Critics have argued that the instrument was designed primarily for detecting comparatively high levels of radiation associated with military contamination concerns rather than for precise measurement of low-level environmental background radiation. If so, small variations near the lower end of its scale become difficult to interpret confidently. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/SUNlite%20Rendlesham%20radiation.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">can have natu- rally occurring radioactive.Read more...</span></span></span>

## What the Readings Can Prove

The radiation evidence can support several limited conclusions.

First, it confirms that Halt's team conducted radiation checks during the investigation and recorded numerical results rather than merely claiming that radiation was present. The readings are documented in the memorandum and echoed in the contemporaneous audio recordings often known as the Halt Tape. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

Second, the readings indicate that measurable differences existed between some points in the surveyed area. The depressions and part of the nearby tree produced readings that investigators considered noteworthy enough to record. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Ian RidpathAppendix – Col Halt&#x27;s memo to the MoDA nearby tree had moderate (0.05–0.07) readings on the side of the tree toward the depres...</span></span></span>

Third, the measurements help explain why the incident attracted attention beyond a simple report of lights in the sky. Physical traces and instrument readings gave the case an evidential dimension that many UFO reports lack. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/explore-the-collection/explore-by-time-period/postwar/ufo-reports/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: nationalarchives.gov.uk">[The National Archives]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">nationalarchives.gov.uk</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The National ArchivesUFO reportsEarly letters regarding UFO sightings · Correspondence on the Rendlesham Forest incident · Documents on U...</span></span></span>

These are legitimate evidential points. They show that the investigation involved more than eyewitness testimony alone.

## What the Readings Cannot Prove

The radiation data cannot establish that an unidentified craft landed in Rendlesham Forest.

The measurements do not identify a source. Radiation can originate from natural environmental variation, local soil composition, contamination from ordinary human activity, or other causes. The readings themselves contain no information capable of distinguishing among those possibilities. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/SUNlite%20Rendlesham%20radiation.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: ianridpath.com">[Ian Ridpath]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">ianridpath.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">can have natu- rally occurring radioactive.Read more...</span></span></span>

The data also cannot demonstrate that the site had been exposed to unusually high radiation. Debate over the figures has persisted largely because there was no comprehensive scientific survey establishing what constituted normal background levels at that exact location under controlled conditions. ## How the Readings Shaped Later Claims
